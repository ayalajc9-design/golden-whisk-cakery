Labor & Time

Her time on each cake, turned into a labor cost that is built into the cake's total cost and profit. Saved in this browser on this device only, together with Cake costing.

1. Hourly rate

Set this once. It is what an hour of her work is worth, and it applies to every cake.

2. Time per cake

How long each cake takes from start to finish: baking, decorating, cleanup. Cakes are added on the Cake costing tab.

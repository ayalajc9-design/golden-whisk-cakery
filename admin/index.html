Back office

The working side of Golden Whisk Cakery. Customers never see these pages; they are not linked from the public site.

Orders & customers

Add and update orders, see what is due this week, track deposits and balances, link each order to a costed cake to see its profit, and keep notes on customers.

Cake costing

One fixed cost that applies to every cake, plus the custom ingredients and labor for each cake, giving total cost and profit.

Labor & time

Her hourly rate and the time each cake takes. The labor cost is built into every cake's total cost and profit.

Marketing

The printable menu flyer and ready-to-copy social media posts.

Public website

What customers see: the menu, how to order, and contact details.

How saving works

Orders and costing are saved in the browser on the device you type them on. They do not appear on another phone or computer by themselves. Use "Download backup" and "Load backup" on each page to copy them across, and download a backup now and then in case the browser's data is cleared.
